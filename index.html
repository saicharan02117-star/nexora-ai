MANDATORY RESPONSIBLE AI

Useful AI must also be trustworthy.

NEXUS-Ω follows four responsible-AI requirements from the internship guideline: fairness, transparency, ethics and privacy.

F

Fairness

Do not disadvantage people because of identity, language, income or neighborhood. Vulnerability can guide support priority, but not discriminatory treatment.

T

Transparency

Every important output explains the evidence used, missing information, confidence and whether data is live, user-entered or simulated.

E

Ethics

NEXUS-Ω does not fabricate emergencies and does not autonomously execute consequential safety actions. Human judgment and official instructions remain primary.

P

Privacy

Family data stays local in the prototype. Exact location is not sent to the optional external AI; only minimized weather context is shared.
