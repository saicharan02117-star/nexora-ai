# Product Vision

## Zero-App Commerce

Nexora is designed to become the customer-facing coordination layer above merchants, inventory, payment, fulfilment and support systems.

The long-term experience is simple:

> Tell Nexora what you want done, not which application to use.

## Universal Intent Marketplace

Traditional marketplaces ask customers to search through inventory. Nexora's future Intent Marketplace reverses the direction: a user or business publishes a structured requirement and eligible merchant systems respond with compliant offers.

Example:

> Need 25 laptops under ₹15 lakh total, delivered before Friday.

Nexora would normalize the requirement, collect eligible proposals, compare price, availability and delivery constraints, and return the best plan for approval.
