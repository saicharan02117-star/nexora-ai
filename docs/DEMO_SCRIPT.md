# NEXUS-Ω Demo Script (3-4 Minutes)

## 0:00-0:30 - Problem
“Most systems tell us one thing at a time: weather, traffic, electricity or water. But real disruptions are connected. During extreme rain, a road problem can affect access to a substation; power disruption can affect a water pump; and that can affect hospital continuity. NEXUS-Ω is designed to understand that chain and help users decide what to do next.”

## 0:30-1:10 - Personal Resilience
Open **Today**.
- Enable location.
- Show live weather.
- Point to the weather risk indicator.
- Open **Why this result?**

Say:
“NEXUS-Ω separates live weather from data it does not have. It never claims live road closures or utility outages without a verified source.”

## 1:10-1:40 - Ask NEXUS
Open **Ask NEXUS**.

Ask:
**Can I travel now?**

Say:
“The assistant first tries a free community AI. If that service is unavailable, the local safety fallback still produces a transparent answer using the loaded weather context. No paid OpenAI or Perplexity key is required.”

## 1:40-2:10 - Route + Family
Open **Route Guardian**.
- Enter a destination.
- Click Check trip.

Open **Family Guardian**.
- Add a sample family member.
- Mark checked in.

Say:
“The prototype keeps these personal items local on the device.”

## 2:10-3:10 - Cascade Lab
Open **Cascade Lab**.

Say:
“This is a synthetic digital-twin demonstration. The values are not live city data.”

Show:
**Extreme Rain → Road Access → Substation 4 → Pump 2 → Hospital A**

Click **Apply recommended intervention**.

Say:
“Ω-CORE compares counterfactual interventions and updates downstream modeled risk. This demonstrates Break-the-Chain reasoning: find a high-leverage place to interrupt a cascading failure.”

Show the Climate, Power, Water, Health and Verification agents.

## 3:10-3:40 - Responsible AI
Open **Responsible AI**.

Say:
“The project follows four mandatory principles: fairness, transparency, ethics and privacy. Every important data point is labeled as live, local, simulated or unverified.”

## 3:40-4:00 - Closing
“NEXUS-Ω is not claiming to be a production city control system. It is a responsible AI prototype showing how personal resilience, causal reasoning and infrastructure dependencies can be brought into one explainable decision-support system aligned with SDG 11.”
