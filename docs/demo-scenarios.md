# Demo Scenarios

## Scenario 1 — Product Purchase

Prompt:

`Find me the best laptop below ₹70,000 for AI development and coding.`

Expected demonstration:

1. Structured intent extraction
2. Eligible inventory discovery
3. Performance-aware ranking
4. Merchant-authorized offer
5. Budget status
6. Agent Firewall confirmation requirement
7. Test-order creation

## Scenario 2 — Multi-Merchant Birthday Mission

Prompt:

`Arrange a birthday for 25 people under ₹20,000.`

Expected demonstration:

1. Event intent recognition
2. People and budget extraction
3. Vendor discovery across food, cake, decor and photography
4. Lowest-cost eligible bundle
5. Total and remaining budget
6. Approval before transaction

## Scenario 3 — Merchant Revenue Intelligence

Prompt:

`Why is my merchant revenue leaking today?`

Expected demonstration:

1. Revenue today
2. Potential revenue
3. Recoverable leakage
4. Leakage categories
5. Recommended next-best recovery actions
